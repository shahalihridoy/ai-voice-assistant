# Riverside Clinic

Riverside Clinic is a neighborhood outpatient clinic.

Address: 120 Harbor Street, Riverside.

Main phone: 555-0142.

Front desk hours: Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM. Saturday, 9 AM to 1 PM. Closed Sunday.

The clinic does not provide emergency care. For chest pain, trouble breathing, or severe bleeding, call emergency services.
